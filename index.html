Where Your Smile Comes First.

Oradent Dental Clinic is a dental care facility located at Usman Plaza, Main GT Road, Gujar Khan. We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to care for their oral health in a comfortable and reassuring environment.

Our approach is centred on listening to patients, understanding their dental concerns and helping them explore suitable care options. Whether you need general dental attention or are considering restorative treatment, we're here to help you take an informed next step.
