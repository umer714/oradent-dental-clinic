Privacy Notice

A simple explanation of how this informational website handles your interaction.

Information you provide

The appointment enquiry form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. This static website does not itself submit or store the information you enter. If you send a WhatsApp message, your interaction is then handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy terms.

Calls and external services

When you use phone links, Google Maps links or WhatsApp links, you leave this website or open the relevant application. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently provide a custom account system or a website-hosted appointment database. If analytics, advertising or additional tracking tools are added later, this notice should be updated before those tools are enabled.

Medical information

Please do not send sensitive medical records or urgent medical information through a general website enquiry. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for advice specific to your situation.

Contact

For clinic enquiries, contact Oradent Dental Clinic at +92 332 1918505 or +92 307 8938817.

For questions about this website notice or your enquiry, please contact the clinic directly.
